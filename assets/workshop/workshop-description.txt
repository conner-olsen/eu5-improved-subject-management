[h1]Overview:[/h1]
Adds new interactions, modifications, and automations to significantly improve subject management.

[h1]Features:[/h1]
[olist]
 [*][b]New Interactions to Transfer land betwen subjects[/b] - Allows you to make nice borders.
	[list]
	 [*][b]Transfer to:[/b] Give your subject land, either from another subject or yourself.
	[*][b]Transfer from:[/b] Take land from your subject, and give it to another subject or yourself (*if taking from a colonial nation).
	[/list]
 [*][b]New interaction to change the capital of subjects[/b] - Sometimes they pick terrible locations, or you create a subject with a poor capital location initially and want to fix it later.
 [*][b]New interaction to change the Liturgical Language of your subject[/b] - And adds a decaying -15 loyalty and 1000% change cost to the subject, preventing them from immediately switching away.
 [*][b]New interaction to block and unblock a subject from colonizing[/b]
 [*][b]New interaction to block and unblock a subject from urbanizing[/b] - Prevents them from founding towns.
 [*][b]New automatic map sharing feature[/b] - Every year, any area discovered by a subject and not by its overlord that borders an area discovered by its overlord spreads to the overlord. This will steadily spread all subject maps to their overlords.
 [*][b]Block colonial subjects with the same overlord from declaring war on each other.[/b]
 [*][b]You can now enforce peace on your colonial subjects.[/b]
 [*][b]You can select the size of the land to be transferred: Location, Province, Area, Region[/b]
 [*][b]Remove location limitation on what land can be transferred[/b] - In vanilla, you will commonly be unable to give a location one province away to your subjects.
 [*][b]You can freely transfer land between colonial subjects and yourself.[/b] - You gave them the land to begin with, and in most cases, it's not better to own colonial land yourself, so this shouldn't have any effect on game balance.
 [*][b]Being at war no longer fully blocks managing the land of subjects[/b] - Why should being at war with a one-province minor halfway across the world prevent me from giving my newly colonized land to my subject? (You can transfer land between subjects that are in the same war, and you can always transfer land from a colonial nation.)
[/olist]

[h1]Translations:[/h1]
My mod includes auto-generated translation using DeepL for all natively supported EU5 languages. I am aware that there [b]will[/b] be mistakes in translation. This is not meant to be a replacement for native translations, rather a supplement for when they are not available (a better-than-nothing approach).

[h2]Auto-Translated Localizations:[/h2]
[list]
 [*][b]Chinese / 中文[/b]
 [*][b]French / Français[/b]
 [*][b]German / Deutsch[/b]
 [*][b]Japanese / 日本語[/b]
 [*][b]Korean / 한국어[/b]
 [*][b]Polish / Polski[/b]
 [*][b]Portuguese (Brazil) / Português (Brasil)[/b]
 [*][b]Russian / Русский[/b]
 [*][b]Spanish / Español[/b]
 [*][b]Turkish / Türkçe[/b]
[/list]
Note that I do not natively speak any of these languages, and I automate the translation process, so I won't create fixes for the above translations if there are any issues. However, if you would like to fix an issue yourself, you can submit a PR on GitHub, and I will approve it.

In addition to the auto-translation, native (human-generated) localizations are available for some languages.

[h2]Native Localizations:[/h2]
[list]
 [*][b]English:[/b] Primary
 [*][b]Chinese / 中文:[/b] Available in the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3599706198]凯撒汉化 (Caesar Chinese Translation) mod[/url]. (Thanks to [i]牛奶大魔王[/i]). Make sure you load this mod after mine to overwrite my existing auto-translations.
[/list]
Chinese speakers should still use 凯撒汉化, as it provides far superior translations, just make sure to load it after my mod to ensure it overrides the generated ones. I only included the auto-translations for Chinese so that if a new localization is added, this will act as a stopgap until 凯撒汉化 adds a better one (or if they decide it's good enough, then it will be less work for them).
